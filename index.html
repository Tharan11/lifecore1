Exercise library

Move gently, use a comfortable range and stop if you feel pain, dizzy or unwell.

Bodyweight squat

Leg strength

Stand comfortably. Bend hips and knees as if sitting back, then stand smoothly.

Easier option: sit-to-stand from a stable chair.

Wall push-up

Upper body

Hands on wall at chest height. Keep body aligned, bend elbows gently, then press away.

Keep the movement controlled.

Bird dog

Core stability

On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg only as far as comfortable. Avoid twisting.

Glute bridge

Hip strength

Lie on your back with knees bent. Gently lift hips, then lower with control.

Easy marching

Warm-up

March in place at an easy pace, moving arms naturally.

Gentle mobility

Mobility

Move shoulders, ankles and hips slowly through comfortable ranges. Do not force a stretch.

These are general examples, not rehabilitation advice. Ask a qualified professional about exercise with injury or ongoing symptoms.

Health & safety guide

🏃 Exercise safely

  • Warm up gently and cool down.
  • Build activity gradually and include recovery days.
  • Choose comfortable footwear and safe routes.
  • Stop if you feel unwell, dizzy or have unusual pain.

🥗 Everyday nutrition

  • Eat a variety of foods across food groups.
  • Include fruits, vegetables, grains and protein foods.
  • Eat regular meals that fit your appetite and routine.
  • Avoid extreme diets and unnecessary food rules.

☀️ Heat & hydration

  • Prefer cooler times for outdoor activity in hot weather.
  • Take shade and rest breaks.
  • Drink regularly and pay attention to thirst.
  • Stop and seek help if faint or confused.

🧼 Hygiene & recovery

  • Wash hands before eating and after using the toilet.
  • Brush teeth twice daily.
  • Clean reusable bottles regularly.
  • Prioritize adequate sleep and rest.
Urgent: severe breathing difficulty, fainting, chest pain, confusion or a serious allergic reaction needs immediate help from a trusted adult and emergency medical services. LIFECORE is not a medical device.

Trusted references: WHO physical activity · WHO healthy diet.
